02 Platforms

Five systems. One standard.

From onboarding a new broker to routing an order across fourteen venues, each platform is built to run unattended in production.

An AI-assisted certification platform that reads counterparty specifications, generates test packs and verifies conformance on its own. Onboarding goes from six weeks to a few days.

  • Up to 80% less time to onboard clients and brokers
  • Parses custom specs, dictionaries and repeating groups
  • Self-service testing portal with conformance certificates

FIXatdl work across the whole lifecycle: schema design, strategy authoring, parameter rule trees and UI rendering in the major OMS and EMS platforms.

  • FIXatdl 1.1 / 1.2 compliant strategy XML
  • Parameter validation and widget mapping
  • Bloomberg EMSX, Fidessa, Enfusion, Charles River

A deterministic messaging engine in C++20 and low-GC Java, built for high-throughput order routing with zero-allocation memory pools and lock-free queues.

  • Sub-microsecond dispatch (< 1.12µs)
  • FIX 4.0 through 5.0SP2 and FAST
  • Multi-asset session management

A rule-based router that brings together fragmented liquidity, crosses in midpoint dark pools and optimises fees across lit and alternative venues while keeping market impact low.

  • NBBO sweep with dark midpoint crossing
  • Fee-aware venue ranking
  • Configurable routing goals per desk

Real-time packet telemetry, drop-copy reconciliation after the trade, sequence reset alerts and anomaly detection that catch problems before they become outages.

  • Works with any FIX engine or venue
  • Post-trade drop-copy reconciliation
  • Session and sequence anomaly alerts
